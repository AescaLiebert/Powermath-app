# Math:World Animo Gacha Art Direction — v10

## Supersession notice

This v10 direction supersedes the **visual art direction** of the v9 Animo gacha mockups.

- The moon-fox, leaf-drake, ember-ram, observatory garden, and moonlit pool from v9 are rejected concepts. They are not Math:World canon and must not be reused as actual pet designs.
- The user's supplied pet images are **art-style references only**. Their species, anatomy, markings, accessories, colors, and identities must never be copied into Math:World.
- The interaction and transaction rules in `animo-gacha-sequence-v9.md` remain useful, but the visual system is replaced by this document.
- All creatures shown in the v10 gacha mockups are deliberately non-canon geometric placeholders used to evaluate UI composition and rendering only. Replace them with approved Animo assets later without changing the UI hierarchy.

## Target style

The target sits between smooth youthful anime rendering and shape-driven cartoony illustration.

### Animo illustration

- Begin with one unmistakable silhouette and two or three large color masses.
- Use flat markings as graphic cutouts. Do not model every surface or strand.
- Outer contours may be slightly firmer than the heroine's, but should be soft indigo, brown, or local color rather than black.
- Interior lines are thinner and sparse. Use them only for expression, overlap, or one identity mark.
- Apply one hard shadow shape, a tiny soft contact/AO shadow, and selective gradients around eyes or one focal marking.
- Add subtle paper or pencil grain across flat fills; keep the texture quiet and consistent.
- Expression and pose provide appeal. Avoid compensating with fur, particle, armor, or lighting detail.

### Human character illustration

- Use a 5.5–6-head body ratio: smaller head, longer neck, longer torso and legs, slender athletic limbs, and natural hands and feet.
- Keep the heroine youthful and friendly, but not chibi, toddler-like, or adult-glamorous.
- Concentrate detail in eyes, brows, mouth, front hair, compass accessory, and hands.
- Use very thin local-color contours: warm brown on skin and hair, desaturated indigo on blue clothing, and pale blue-gray on cream clothing.
- The outer contour is only slightly heavier than internal lines. Allow bright edges to disappear into the light.
- Use one hard cel shadow plus a few soft gradients on the face, front hair, jacket, and major cloth folds. AO remains lightly saturated blue-violet or muted peach.

## Gacha background system

The gacha sequence uses a **flat graphic stage**, not an illustrated location.

Allowed background ingredients:

- two- or three-color gradients;
- oversized cropped low-contrast words such as `ANIMO`, `CALL`, `NEW`, or `RESULT`;
- halftone-dot discs and checker-dot clusters;
- broad cream, cobalt, and coral arcs;
- compass diamonds, paw-dot clusters, and thin concentric rings;
- subtle paper grain.

Do not use:

- gardens, observatories, pools, buildings, trees, water, horizons, or landscapes;
- cinematic depth-of-field or realistic atmospheric perspective;
- galaxy fields, particle storms, or environment lighting that competes with the UI;
- a unique ornamental frame for every state.

The same shapes should recompose across screens so the sequence feels animated without changing visual language.

## Screen rules

### Banner

- Calm title zone on the left; featured art cluster on the right.
- Keep only `ANIMO CALL`, one guarantee line, currency, close, `DETAILS`, `HISTORY`, and x1/x10 actions.
- `DETAILS` and `HISTORY` use labeled cream secondary buttons. They are discoverable but never compete with the warm primary x10 button.
- Large cropped `ANIMO` typography and halftone shapes replace scenery.

### Summon transition

- A central compass emblem and ten evenly spaced capsules communicate x10.
- Exactly nine cyan capsules and one gold capsule foreshadow at least one rare outcome.
- Capsules remain sealed; no pet silhouette appears before reveal.
- Only `SKIP` remains interactive.

### Rare reveal

- One Animo, one compass burst, one rarity row, one short label, and one continue action.
- Use the Animo's expression and pose as the primary event. Keep glow secondary.
- Do not invent a canon name in a layout study; use `FEATURED ANIMO` until approved data exists.

### Result

- Exactly ten equal cards in a 5×2 grid.
- One `NEW` ribbon and restrained gold edge distinguish the featured result; never enlarge that card.
- Element chip, portrait, and rarity diamonds are the only card content.
- The background remains a flat pattern field, not a dark cave or location.

## Validation checklist

- Reference separation: can every Animo be distinguished from the species and anatomy in supplied references?
- Silhouette: can each portrait be identified when reduced to a solid shape?
- Value grouping: does each Animo still read with only two or three large masses?
- Line check: are human contours visibly thinner than v8 and pet interiors thinner than their outer contour?
- Background check: does removing the UI reveal graphic design rather than a physical scene?
- Mobile check: are title, costs, Details, History, rarity, and result count readable at reduced size?
- Count check: x10 transition and result both visibly contain exactly ten units.
